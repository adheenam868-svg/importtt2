The Roof of the World

Nestled in the lap of the mighty Himalayas, Nepal is a sovereign nation brimming with striking natural beauty, rich biodiversity, and deep-rooted spiritual heritage. From towering snowy summits to lush tropical lowlands, it offers a breathtaking tapestry of landscapes.

Majestic Peaks

Home to eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including Mt. Everest, drawing trekkers and climbers from across the globe.

Rich Heritage

A living museum featuring ancient palaces, intricately carved pagodas, and vibrant festivals rooted in Hindu and Buddhist traditions.

Diverse Wildlife

Subtropical jungles in the south harbor rare and endangered species like the Bengal tiger, one-horned rhinoceros, and Asian elephant.
